R/M/A share speed, travel time and delay; M/A share VSP. VSP compares operating-state exposure averages, not VSP evaluated at equivalent speed.

Average speed = total distance / total vehicle-hours. S matches the observed episode average by construction. The fitted minimum speed is the calibrated peak target, before five-minute averaging.

Free/queue times and distances have no independent observed-state reference, so no error is assigned to those panels.

Metric definitions

WAPE = 100 × Σ|estimate − O| / Σ|O| over the same five-minute bins. APE = 100 × |estimate − O| / |O| for one episode value. No additional count weighting is applied to WAPE.

Delay = travel time − L/vf; signed observed delays are retained. A zero reference denominator is undefined (—). Full MAE, RMSE and signed bias are in the CSV.
