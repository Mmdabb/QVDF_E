How to interpret the comparison

Balanced four minimizes equal-weight squared relative errors, so large-magnitude CO₂ totals do not dominate by unit scale. CO₂ target gives priority only to CO₂. The bounds restrict physically admissible density–wave-speed combinations while holding fitted discharge fixed.

Context for the exploratory bounds

Laval and Daganzo use an example jam density of 150 veh/mi/lane and a 15 mph backward-wave magnitude. The GDOT I-285 study, Table 5 reports 150 veh/km and 20 km/h. Neither example establishes universal limits for the sensitivity box used here.
