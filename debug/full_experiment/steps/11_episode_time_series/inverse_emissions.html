Equations, units and physical constraint

Two-speed emission total

t¯q(q)=vfw¯^vf−q
t¯f(q)=Tf−qw¯^vf−q
Mp(q)=D[t¯f(q)rp(vf)+t¯q(q)rp(q)]

Here q is the candidate queue speed, in mph, rather than traffic flow. State times are hours per vehicle, D is vehicles per lane, and rₚ is grams per vehicle-hour. Mₚ and Oₚ are episode grams per lane. The original speed → VSP → mode lookup supplies the rate; no interpolation smooths its jumps.

Two diagnostic targets

minimizeq|MCO₂(q)OCO₂−1|
J(q)=14∑p(Mp(q)Op−1)2
Normalized RMSE (%)=100J

CO₂ uses the smallest absolute relative CO₂ residual. Balanced four minimizes J across the four pollutants within each episode. The displayed normalized RMSE summarizes this four-pollutant balance, not pooled error across episodes.

Physical speed bound

0<q≤LTf+wpeak^

The bound ensures nonnegative free-state time at the fitted peak delay. D, L, free-flow speed, and fitted mean and peak delay stay fixed. These are conditional inverse calculations, not traffic measurements or a refitted QVDF.

Every constant-rate interval is solved separately. A rate-jump crossing is never counted as a root. Ties select the queue speed nearest the current fitted value. A rate-boundary limit may only be approached; superscript − or + indicates the approach side. Display rounding can hide the small offset, so use the full-precision CSV for calculation.
